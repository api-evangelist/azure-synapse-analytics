Overview The crucial first step in any ETL (extract, transform, load) process or data engineering program is ingestion, which involves dealing with multiple data sources and entities or datasets. Managing ingestion flow for multiple entities is therefore essential. This article outlines a Metadata-based approach for ingestion that allows for convenient modification of datasets whenever necessary.
